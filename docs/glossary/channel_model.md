# Channel Model
---

## Social Commerce


**Definition**  
A digital commerce model where selling activities occur directly within social media platforms through content sharing, community interaction, and informal promotion, without a structured transaction or fulfillment system.

**Examples**  
Facebook Pages, Facebook Groups, Instagram Pages

## Peer-to-Peer Listings

**Definition**  
A model where individuals publish simple listings to sell goods or services directly to other individuals, typically within a localized digital marketplace, without seller branding or long-term presence.

**Examples**  
Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, Leboncoin

## Direct Messaging Commerce

**Definition**  
A commerce model based on one-to-one or small-group messaging, where discovery, negotiation, and transactions occur through direct communication rather than a public marketplace.

**Examples**  
WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram

## Messaging with Catalog (Semi-Structured Commerce)

**Definition**  
An extension of direct messaging commerce that introduces lightweight product catalogs, labels, and business profiles, while remaining informal and conversation-driven.

**Examples**  
WhatsApp Business
