# Type of Governance
---

## Collective Governance

**Definition**  
A governance model where decision-making authority is shared among members of a group or cooperative, typically based on democratic principles such as shared ownership and collective voting.

**Examples**  
Artisan cooperatives, producer associations, mutual aid groups.

## Institutional Governance

**Definition**  
A governance model led by formal organizations such as NGOs, public institutions, or international agencies, where decisions are centralized and guided by programs, policies, and funding frameworks.

**Examples**  
NGO-led platforms, donor-funded development programs, public-sector initiatives.


## Hybrid / Platform Governance

**Definition**  
A governance model where a digital platform provides structure, rules, and visibility while allowing communities, cooperatives, or partners to self-organize within defined boundaries.

**Examples**  
Open platforms, federated networks, community-enabled digital ecosystems.
