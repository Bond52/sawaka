# Monetization Models — Sawaka Glossary

This document defines monetization models used in Sawaka’s analysis, requirements, and strategic discussions.

---

## Transaction-Based Monetization

**Definition**  
Monetization based on commissions or fees charged per transaction executed on or through the platform.

**Examples**  
Etsy, Uber, Airbnb

**Implications for Sawaka**  
Not suitable at launch due to legal, technical, and inclusion constraints.

---

## Subscription-Based Monetization

**Definition**  
Recurring payments granting access to premium features or services.

**Examples**  
Shopify, LinkedIn Premium

**Implications for Sawaka**  
Potential long-term option for B2B or institutional services.

---

## Advertising-Based Monetization

**Definition**  
Revenue generated from displaying advertisements to users.

**Examples**  
Facebook, Google

**Implications for Sawaka**  
Not aligned with community trust and mission.

---

## Value-Added Services

**Definition**  
Monetization through complementary services independent of sales transactions.

**Examples**  
Training, branding, analytics, certification

**Implications for Sawaka**  
Strong candidate aligned with impact-driven strategy.

---

## Partnership-Based Monetization

**Definition**  
Funding through partnerships with NGOs, institutions, or sponsors.

**Examples**  
Grants, sponsored programs

**Implications for Sawaka**  
Highly relevant at launch.

---

## Data-Driven Monetization

**Definition**  
Use of aggregated and anonymized data for research or institutional insights.

**Examples**  
Market studies, trend reports

**Implications for Sawaka**  
Long-term option requiring strong governance.
