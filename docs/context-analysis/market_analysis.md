## 1️⃣ Artisan Marketplaces

| Platform | Model | Target Users | Monetization | Strengths | Weaknesses | Website |
|--------|-------|--------------|--------------|-----------|------------|---------|
| Etsy | Global transactional marketplace | Formal artisans, creatives | Listing fees + commissions | Global reach, strong buyer trust, mature ecosystem | High fees, strict rules, formalization required, highly competitive, limited local relevance, weak location-based discovery | https://www.etsy.com |
| Anka | SaaS + Africa-focused marketplace | African brands, designers, and structured artisans | Subscription + commissions | Brand-building tools, export-oriented, cultural storytelling | Complex onboarding, cost barriers, export and logistics complexity, high competition, limited local market discovery | https://marketplace.anka.africa/en |

Artisan marketplaces assume seller formalization, solvable demand, and logistics maturity. Most are primarily designed for export-oriented sales to external markets, which limits their relevance for artisans operating in low–purchasing-power local contexts. These platforms are therefore complementary rather than competitive to Sawaka. Sawaka addresses an upstream gap by strengthening local visibility, community coordination, and regional opportunity discovery, enabling artisans to collaborate, respond to local demand, and progressively build capacity before engaging with export-focused marketplaces.


## 2️⃣ Informal Digital Channels

| Channel | Model | Adoption Level | Monetization | Strengths | Weaknesses | Why Artisans Use It |
|--------|-------|---------------|--------------|-----------|------------|--------------------|
| Facebook Pages / Groups | Social commerce | Very high | None / Ads | Visibility, zero entry barrier, network effect, basic geographic and category filtering | Low trust, poor discovery quality, lack of structure | Free and familiar |
| Facebook Marketplace | Peer-to-peer listings | High | None | Local reach, simplicity, basic geographic and category filtering | No artisan identity, no quality or skill validation | Fast exposure |
| WhatsApp | Direct messaging | Extremely high | None | Direct contact, high trust, low data usage | No public discoverability, no scalability | Relationship-based sales |
| WhatsApp Business | Messaging + catalog | Growing | None | Product catalog, labels, structured chats, low data usage | No public search engine, no discovery by location or profession, reach limited to existing contacts | Semi-structured selling |

Informal digital channels excel in accessibility and low entry barriers, but consistently fail on visibility, structure, and scalability.

Despite high adoption, many Facebook artisan groups exhibit very low engagement. Large member counts often do not translate into interaction when content is predominantly institutional or informational, with limited opportunities for collaboration, training, or peer-to-peer mentorship.

As a result, these channels tend to generate passive audiences rather than active, self-sustaining communities.

## 3️⃣ Cooperative & Community-Based Models

| Model | Governance | Strengths | Limitations | Examples (Cameroon) |
|------|------------|-----------|-------------|--------------------|
| Artisan Association | Collective | Strong trust, shared identity, mutual support | Limited reach, offline-heavy, weak digital visibility | https://www.facebook.com/groups/1759961094355728/ |
| NGO-led platforms | Institutional | Training, funding access, structuring of value chains | Not product-driven, slow execution, project-based | FAO Cameroon — https://www.fao.org/cameroon/en |
| Community networks | Informal | Cultural alignment, strong social ties | No digital tooling, no discovery, no scalability | Informal artisan groups and community associations documented by Afrobarometer — https://www.afrobarometer.org/about/our-network/ |

Cooperatives and community-based models excel at trust and social cohesion. Sawaka can support them by enhancing digital visibility, structured discovery, and market access at scale through partnerships and a shared platform infrastructure.
